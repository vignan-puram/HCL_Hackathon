Data Retention Policy
=====================

Version 1.2
Effective date: 2025-05-01

1. Scope

This policy explains how long Nimbus Workspace keeps customer data after it has been deleted by a user or an admin, and how backups are handled. It applies to files, messages, workspaces and audit logs.

2. Retention Periods by Plan

Enterprise plan: deleted workspace data is retained for 180 days before permanent deletion. This extended period gives Enterprise customers more time to recover data and to meet internal compliance reviews.

Free and Pro plans: deleted data follows the standard retention period of 90 days described in the Security FAQ.

3. Backups

Encrypted backups are kept for 35 days. Backups are used only for disaster recovery and cannot be used to restore individual files on request.

4. Legal Hold

Enterprise admins can place a workspace on legal hold. Data under legal hold is never permanently deleted until the hold is released, regardless of the retention period.

5. Audit Logs

Audit logs are retained for 12 months on the Pro plan and 24 months on the Enterprise plan. The Free plan does not include audit logs.
