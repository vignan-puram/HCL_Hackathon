# Security FAQ

Common questions about how Nimbus Workspace protects your data.

## Is multi-factor authentication supported?

Yes. Members can enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) with any authenticator app that supports time-based one-time passwords (TOTP). Workspace admins can make MFA mandatory for all members in Admin Console > Security.

## How is data encrypted?

All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Encryption keys are rotated automatically every year.

## How long is deleted data kept?

Deleted data is retained for 90 days before it is permanently erased. During those 90 days a workspace admin can restore deleted files and workspaces from the trash. After that, the data cannot be recovered.

## Which compliance certifications does Nimbus hold?

Nimbus Workspace is audited annually against SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001. Audit reports are available to Enterprise customers on request.

## How do I report a security vulnerability?

Send details to the security team through the responsible disclosure form. The team acknowledges reports within two business days.
