# Billing FAQ

Last updated 2023-06-10. Answers to common questions about invoices, payment methods and refunds for Nimbus Workspace.

## How do I update my payment card?

Workspace owners can update the payment card in Admin Console > Billing > Payment methods. The new card is used for the next invoice; past invoices are not charged again.

## Refunds

You can request a refund within 14 days of your annual renewal. Refund requests are submitted from Admin Console > Billing > Request refund, and approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Monthly plans are not eligible for refunds.

## Do you offer discounts for non-profit organisations?

Yes. Registered non-profit organisations receive a 25% discount on the Pro plan. Contact the sales team with proof of non-profit status to apply the discount.

## Which currencies can I pay in?

Invoices can be paid in US dollars, euros or British pounds. The currency is chosen when the subscription is created and cannot be changed afterwards.

## Where can I download invoices?

All invoices are available as PDF files in Admin Console > Billing > Invoices. Invoices are kept for seven years.
